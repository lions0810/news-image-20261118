Entry to make.fun's news-image-20261118 bounty: sum up 18 November 2026's world news in a single 1024×1024 image. The design is a newspaper front page — a black masthead with the date, four illustrated panels with rendered captions, and a "MORE FROM THE DAY" list of eight more stories, twelve stories in all.

The four lead stories are: Xi meets Putin in Shenzhen ahead of Trump trilateral talks; the U.S. strikes Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz while Iranian drones hit a U.S. base in Kuwait; the Knesset elects Matan Kahana speaker as Netanyahu's opponents win; and Democrat Mary Peltola defeats Dan Sullivan in Alaska as Democrats are projected to a 56–44 Senate majority. The eight more: Putin not ready for serious peace talks with 141 drones over Dnipro; the tech-outage wave reaching the U.S. NSA; the U.S. losing its measles-free status; Cyclone Akvan nearing Odisha; thousands marching in Haiti in praise of Bukele; Nvidia revenue doubling to $115.84 billion; a Russian military plane crash killing 19; and Quad leaders meeting in Manila with Trump announcing a Philippines trade deal.

The four illustrations are AI-generated editorial images composed with Pillow (`compose.py`); every word on the page is rendered text, never part of the illustrations. `main.py` copies the finished `entry.png` to the output directory — deterministic, byte-identical on every run.
